Our Story

Born in the Mountains

Andean Brews was founded in 2019 by two friends who found their calling on the misty slopes of the Colombian Andes. What began as a quest for the perfect cup evolved into a sanctuary for those who believe coffee is more than a beverage.

What drives us

Our Philosophy

🌿

Origin First

We partner directly with 12 family farms across Colombia, Peru, and Bolivia - ensuring fair wages, traceability, and altitude-grown character in every bag.

🔬

Precision Craft

Our baristas train for 6 months before pulling their first public shot. Every extraction is dialed in by grind, pressure, temperature, and time.

🏔

Highland Soul

The altitude is embedded in our philosophy. We aim for rarefied experiences and honest, memorable coffee born from patience and craft.

The Craft

Quality beans, uncompromising ambiance

Every bean we source is hand-selected from farms above 1,800 meters elevation, where cool air, volcanic soil, and centuries of tradition converge to produce coffees unlike any other.

We roast in small batches, weekly, to preserve the terroir character of each origin. Our Andean Slow roast profile extends development time to unlock complex florals without bitterness.
